A few good questions

Before you get started.

Who is Entrywell for?

Freelancers, independent professionals, and small business owners who want a straightforward way to organize income, expenses, and receipts. The planned tax export focuses on U.S. Schedule C reporting; it will not cover every business tax situation.

Will I need to connect my bank?

No bank connection is planned for the first release. You’ll be able to add entries yourself, scan receipts, or use Pro to import bank files in CSV, OFX, or QFX format.

Which devices will it support?

The planned minimums are iOS 26, iPadOS 26, and macOS 26. Some on-device intelligence features require compatible hardware. A simpler receipt parser is planned for devices where the language model is unavailable.

Is Entrywell available to download?

Not yet. Entrywell is in development. This page will link directly to the App Store when it is ready. The interface shown above is a design preview.
